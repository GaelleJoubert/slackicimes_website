In 2011, slackline came into my life and gradually took up a big place. Already used to cliffs thanks to climbing, I naturally moved on to highline.

After a difficult start, highline ended up changing me. It changed how I deal with fear, how I see life, and the people around me.

It perfectly meets two of my needs: **understanding & sharing**.

Understanding the technical rigging and the constant evolution of gear, but also fear, pushing yourself, acceptance, and all the other feelings we go through. And sharing my knowledge, seeing the sparkle in the eyes of people discovering slackline, and working with them on what holds them back.

Since then, I have travelled a lot, spent time in the mountains, rigged highlines at many festivals, and run countless introductions.

After several years as president of the Grenoble slackline association, I met Chloë, and our shared values combined with our complementary skills gave life to new projects and experiences, which confirm once again that **slackline can change lives**.
