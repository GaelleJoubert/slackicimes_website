I discovered highline in its very early days in France when I met the Flying Frenchies, pioneers of the sport, and watched the discipline evolve over the years: rigging, gear, techniques...

The beauty of highline made me want to reach alpine places, so I spent several years climbing and mountaineering to be able to rig the lines I dreamed of.

Those years of learning brought as much inner work as technical and physical work, and I became passionate about passing on my experience: first for several years as a festival volunteer, then since 2019 through introduction courses with Gaëlle.

The magic that happens in the highline community is contagious and lasts over the years, and my passion has not aged a bit, unlike me ;)
