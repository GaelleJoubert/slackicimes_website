Slackline came into my life when I was 18. I first fell for longline, before moving on to highline.

My first highline experience was very (too?) emotional. I went home shaking, thinking it was not for me. In the end, surrounded by the right people, I pushed past it, and this passion quickly guided my life.

I love the intense flow you can reach in this sport, the artistic side of our practice and the passionate community around slackline.

Highline made me rediscover mountain sports and see my surroundings with new eyes. I love mixing my passion with other mountain activities: climbing, ski touring, mountaineering, maybe paragliding soon...

Without knowing it, I was always doing "highline freestyle" before the discipline had a name. I have watched it evolve over the years and I hope to share it with as many people as possible. It offers another vision of highline and lets you push yourself at every level!
