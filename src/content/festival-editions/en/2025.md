In the heart of the Les Arcs ski area lies the fabulous natural site of the Deux Têtes, with a panoramic view over the Haute Tarentaise valley and the Mont Blanc massif.

This is where highliners from all over the world gathered, from beginners to the best international athletes, in a friendly atmosphere with the neon colors of the 80s!

This year the spotlight was on surf highline: an impressive and hypnotic discipline! The show was visible to every skier passing the top of the Mont Blanc chairlift.

For introductions, the general public could join the gathering and activities on the place du Soleil in Arc 1600.
