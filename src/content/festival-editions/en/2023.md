Once again, the Deux Têtes spot welcomed highliners from all over the world, from beginners to the best international athletes, in a friendly atmosphere with the neon colors of the 80s!

The spotlight was on **highline freestyle**: a fast growing discipline, with the world's best athletes present for this stage of the World Cup!

A speed competition (speedline) took place on the two longest lines of the site (250 m).

The general public could join the gathering through activities on the place du Soleil in Arc 1600.
