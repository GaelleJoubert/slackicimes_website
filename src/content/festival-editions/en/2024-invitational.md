In the heart of the Les Arcs ski area lies the fabulous natural cirque of Fond Blanc. This year, exceptionally, it hosted the festivities!

The association brought together around fifteen of the world's best athletes to cross the 570 m wide cirque!

During the event, Tania Monier and Tijmen Van Dieren set a new tandem world record (two people crossing together while holding hands).

The athletes made the most of this new playground by combining highline, freestyle skiing (Coline Ballet-Baz) and paragliding, capturing these feats in unique images.

This highline, the longest in Tarentaise, was named Highpowcalypse Snow as a nod to the valley's pioneers.
