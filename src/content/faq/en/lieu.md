---

The courses take place in the Les Arcs resort, in Savoie.

On the first day, we meet at 9 a.m. at the Slackcamp, past the "Tommelet" ski tow in Arc 1600.

Highlining takes place at the Deux Têtes spot, a 10 minute walk from the top of the Cachette chairlift, which leaves from Arc 1600.
