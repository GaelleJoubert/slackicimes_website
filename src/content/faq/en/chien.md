question: "Can I come with my dog?"
---

To reach the highline spot, we take a chairlift, on which dogs are not allowed.

If you still come with your dog, you will need to reach the spot on foot (800 m of elevation gain, a 1.5 to 2 hour walk).

At the SlackCamp, dogs must be kept on a leash.

To make the most of the course, we advise you to have your doggie looked after :)
