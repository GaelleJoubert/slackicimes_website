question: "What does a typical week look like?"
---

A typical week is organized as follows:

- schedule: 9 a.m. to 5 p.m.;
- meeting point: at the bottom of the Cachette chairlift or at the SlackCamp;
- each day takes place either at the Deux Têtes spot or at the SlackCamp;
- one night of bivouac at the Deux Têtes is planned.
