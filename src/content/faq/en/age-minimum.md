question: "What is the minimum age to join the courses?"
---

Our courses are open from 15 years old for young people coming alone, or from 10 years old if accompanied.

We are flexible about age: the most important thing is to feel comfortable in a group made up mostly of adults. To stay at the SlackCamp, participants need to be independent for meals and bedtime, or be accompanied. However, we recommend our [Youth Courses](/en/stages-jeunes), designed especially for participants under 16.
