order: 1
question: "What slackline level do I need?"
---

Our "First Steps" and "Discovery Weekend" courses have no slackline prerequisite. Of course, you will go further if you can already walk on a slackline, but if you cannot, do not worry, we are here to teach you! :)

For the "Autonomy" and "Back 2 Les Arcs" courses, you need to be able to do a sit start on a slackline on the ground, and to have been on a highline at least once.
