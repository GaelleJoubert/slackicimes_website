order: 6
question: "What equipment should I bring?"
---

Collective highline equipment is provided.

We ask trainees to bring their harness and a pair of double lanyards.

For the harness, a climbing harness with a double tie in point is ideal.

The lanyards are simply climbing safety lanyards, to secure yourself on the rock. You can buy them ready made or make them with a piece of rope and two carabiners. Via ferrata lanyards are not suitable.
