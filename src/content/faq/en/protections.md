question: "How can I avoid bruises?"
---

Highlining can cause bruises: better to plan ahead so you can enjoy it fully!

We will have some protection to lend, but you can bring:

- soft knee or ankle pads;
- thick, soft pads (volleyball or dance style);
- an old wetsuit to cut up (for your bottom, arms and thighs);
- cycling shorts, leggings or tights to wear under your trousers.
