question: "How do I organize my accommodation?"
---

The association runs a bivouac area called the SlackCamp. This base camp has toilets and running water. It is accessible by vehicle, and you can stay there in a van or a tent. It is a 15 minute walk from the heart of the resort and the Cachette chairlift.

When you register, you can choose to sleep at the SlackCamp. The ground slackline sessions take place at this base camp.
